# Output Contract

Adapt the structure to the user's requested deliverable, but preserve these semantic elements.

## Required

1. Answer or key findings first.
2. Evidence-backed explanation organized by theme, chronology, or comparison.
3. Material contradictions and uncertainty.
4. Research coverage and limitations when they affect confidence.
5. Source list using headline, publication date, and public URL when available.

## Claim discipline

- Attribute reported facts to their articles.
- Label synthesis, causal interpretation, and forecasts as inference.
- Do not imply that article frequency proves prevalence or importance.
- Preserve uncertainty when sources disagree.
- Do not cite retrieval snippets as article evidence.

## Readability

- Match the user's language and requested level of detail.
- Prefer short paragraphs and descriptive headings.
- Use a timeline or table only when it makes comparison easier.
- Remove repeated setup and process narration from the final answer.
- Keep names, dates, numbers, URLs, and confidence statements unchanged during polishing.

## Optional coverage note

Use a compact note when scope or limitations matter:

```text
Coverage: 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30, 6 monthly windows, 18 full articles read.
Status: partial - one March query failed after retry.
```
